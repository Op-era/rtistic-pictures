About ScriptForge

The Writing Tool

The studio writes with ScriptForge.

ScriptForge exists because of a film. Nature's Trail, this studio's feature, has been in writing for four years, and every draft of it has been written in ScriptForge. The film needed a better writing tool, and that need is part of why Shane Foster built ScriptForge in the first place.

So the studio writes in the tool it sells. The feature, the cartoon episodes, and the lesson films all start as scripts in ScriptForge, and what the writing teaches us goes back into the tool. If you write scripts, the tool is open to you now.

The Working Rules

How we work

Voices first

We record the voices before we animate, and we time the drawings to the sound. The picture follows the performance, never the other way around.

Our own tools

The characters move on AnimAIt, an animation rig we built ourselves. A good action goes into the library and works again in the next episode.

Checked by hand

Every chess position in our films is verified against the real game before anything goes out. Every episode is watched end to end by a person.

The long goal is a feature film. The episodes are where we earn it.
