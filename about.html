About

A working studio, still becoming one.

RTistic Pictures is the studio arm of Haven Command LLC, based in Fresno, California. It was founded by Shane Foster, the founder of Haven Command, to give the company a real film and animation bench.

The Road Here

RTistic Pictures started long before Haven Command.

Shane Foster started making films as a kid, shooting fake commercials and short films with his sister in front of an old VHS camera. He went on to work as a field camera operator for a television station, and he volunteered on the feature film The Visitation, where he learned lighting and camera work from its cinematographer.

He lost his close friend and business partner, Gary Shackelford, and stepped away from the industry for years. In time he made the decision to rebuild Rtistic Pictures and return to the work. Today the studio stands inside Haven Command with the same name and a wider bench: animation, editing, and the tools to do both well.

Two jobs

The studio has two jobs. The first is the cartoon series: original 2D episodes that teach young children, made with an original cast, recorded voices, and a rig we built ourselves. The second is the editing room: chess films and social video for D4 Chess Club, cut to a standard where every position is verified and every move is shown.

Around those two jobs, the studio builds its own tools. When the work needs something that does not exist, we build it, file it in the Haven Command Systems Library, and use it on the next production.

The family

RTistic Pictures sits inside Haven Command next to the products it serves. D4Chess.Club teaches chess with a coach that explains the purpose of a move. ScriptForge is the screenwriting tool the studio writes its stories in. The studio gives both of them a film arm, and they give the studio real work to practice on.

We are a working studio, not a finished one. The series is in production, the tools are young, and the standard rises with every episode. That is the honest state of things, and it is a good place to build from.
